Annual Publishing Plan • Aligned to the Civil Service Hiring Cycle

Every month. Every step of the process. Every future Denton firefighter.

Post copy

One candidate. Every channel. Every month.

This is the annual publishing plan for the Ready to Serve recruiting newsletter and its supporting social media content. It is written for the DFD recruiting team and the City of Denton Marketing and Communications team, the people who put recruiting messages in front of candidates across email, Facebook, LinkedIn, and X.

The twelve themes follow the Civil Service hiring cycle from start to finish. The year opens with the January exam, resets in February with a full career overview, walks candidates through the academy, EMS certification, station life, and the rookie year, then turns toward the next cycle: fitness in September, PAT prep in October, registration in November, and interviews in December. Each month's "Why This Month" box explains the placement. The four weekly cards carry the core message for that week, and beneath each one sits the ready-to-post copy for all three platforms, tuned to each audience and sized for each channel.

The newsletter goes out the first Tuesday of the month and sets the theme. Facebook posts on Monday, LinkedIn on Wednesday, X on Friday. Every month points candidates to one specific recruiting resource. Adjustments to the plan route through the CRR Officer. Use the copy as written or adapt it to the current cycle's dates.

Implementation Notes

  1. Update all date references (exam dates, registration deadlines, academy start dates) at the beginning of each fiscal year. The Key Dates strip at the top of this page and the LINKS block in the page source are the two places to edit.
  2. January edition content should be finalized in December and scheduled in advance to ensure timely delivery before the exam.
  3. Social media posts can be scheduled using Constant Contact's social media scheduler or a third-party tool such as Buffer or Hootsuite. Use the Copy buttons here to move text into the scheduler.
  4. Monitor engagement metrics monthly. If a particular topic or format consistently outperforms others, adjust the calendar accordingly.
  5. Cross-promote content: link newsletter signup in social posts, and include social follow buttons in newsletters.
  6. All content should align with DFD branding standards: professional tone, no em dashes, no AI writing tells, active voice, and concrete language.
  7. Coordinate with the Frontline Report (CRR calendar) so recruiting and community safety posts do not land on the same channel on the same day during high-priority weeks such as Fire Prevention Week or registration deadlines.
